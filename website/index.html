Questions

How much does a villa cost?

₹4,999 per night from Monday to Friday and ₹6,999 per night on Saturday and Sunday.

Can I hold a function or party here?

Yes. We host family functions, birthday parties, small get-togethers and celebrations. Send us your date and group size and we'll plan it with you.

Where is the resort?

On Kava Road, Malampuzha, in Palakkad district, Kerala, close to the Malampuzha reservoir and the Western Ghats.

What is there to do?

Swim in the forest pool, take a coracle ride, try the fish spa, eat in the open-air dining area, and catch the morning mist over the hills at Kava.
